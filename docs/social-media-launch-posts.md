# CMTrace Open Website Launch Posts

## LinkedIn

CMTrace Open finally has a home of its own: [cmtraceopen.com](https://cmtraceopen.com/)

I started CMTrace Open because troubleshooting endpoint logs shouldn’t require archaeology. What began as a better way to investigate Windows logs has grown into a cross-platform workspace for troubleshooting Windows and macOS—and it deserved a proper website.

The new site includes:

- Stable downloads for Windows, macOS, and Linux
- A Field Guide for following real endpoint evidence
- Dedicated nightly builds for testing what’s next
- Clear privacy language: no account required and no runtime telemetry collected by the app

CMTrace Open remains free and open source.

Take a look: [cmtraceopen.com](https://cmtraceopen.com/)
